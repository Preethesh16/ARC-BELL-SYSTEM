# ARC College Bell demo transcript

Narrated walkthrough of the Flutter Windows interface at St Joseph Engineering College, Mangalore. The walkthrough uses simulated data.

## Staff sign-in

This is ARC College Bell, implemented at St Joseph Engineering College, Mangalore. This walkthrough uses sample accounts and an isolated simulation.

## Administrator dashboard

Administrators and developers can see all departments. From this dashboard, open Manage Departments to set up a new department.

## Manage Departments

Mathematics is the existing installation. Departments added through the app can be renamed, assigned a secretary, or retired.

## Add a department

Enter a display name, then save. The app chooses an available department ID. Here, Physics receives department one.

## Create a secretary login

Enter the secretary’s email and an initial password. The account is assigned to Physics. The app sends no invitation email.

## Confirm the setup

Physics now appears alongside Mathematics. A physical bell requires a separately provisioned E S P controller for its department.

## Protect department deletion

Deleting a department requires the administrator’s current password. The old secretary account and controller must be cleared before the ID can be reused. We cancel here.

## Secretary workspace

The Physics secretary sees only Physics. Choose Open bell and schedule to view its bell times.

## Active schedule

The department workspace shows the active schedule and its bell times. Select Add Time to add a scheduled bell.

## Confirm an immediate bell

The manual bell confirmation names the department. We cancel without sending a command. This ends the walkthrough.
